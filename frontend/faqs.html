Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using JobifyPL. Tap a question to see the answer.

Who can use JobifyPL?+
JobifyPL can be used by people looking for employment in Poland, including skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workers, students, temporary workers and people looking for full-time, part-time or seasonal opportunities.
Can I search for a specific type of job?+
Yes. You can search for opportunities according to available job categories, keywords, skills, experience and other filters provided by the application.
Can students use JobifyPL?+
Yes. Students can use JobifyPL to look for suitable employment opportunities, including part-time or seasonal work where available.
Can foreigners use JobifyPL?+
Yes. JobifyPL is designed to connect people looking for employment in Poland with employers and recruitment agencies. Foreign candidates should always check whether they meet the legal requirements for the specific job.
Will the employer contact me directly?+
Depending on the job and the employer's recruitment process, an employer or recruiter may contact you through the available communication methods.
How long does it take to get a response?+
Response times depend on the employer or recruitment agency. JobifyPL does not guarantee a specific response time unless explicitly stated for a particular service.
Can JobifyPL guarantee me a job?+
No. JobifyPL provides a platform to connect candidates with employers and recruitment agencies. Hiring decisions are made by the relevant employer or recruitment agency.
Who can register as an employer?+
Companies and organisations that are legally entitled to recruit employees can use JobifyPL, subject to the platform's registration and verification requirements.
Can I hire someone directly through JobifyPL?+
JobifyPL facilitates connections between candidates and employers. The final hiring process and employment agreement are between the candidate and the employer.
I found a technical problem. What should I do?+
Contact JobifyPL support and provide a description of the problem, screenshots if possible, and the device / app version information requested by support.
