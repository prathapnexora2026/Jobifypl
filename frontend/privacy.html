2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — to provide the Service you sign up for.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — e.g. tax and accounting for payments.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — where you give it (e.g. optional communications).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — security and improving the Service.

3. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: phone number, name, role (candidate/recruiter), email.
  • Profile & documents you choose to upload (e.g. CV/resume, ID documents) — optional.
  • Job and application data (for recruiters and candidates).
  • Payment data: transaction records (processed by our payment provider; we do not store card details).
  • Technical data: device/app usage needed for security and functionality.

4. Purpose of Collecting & Processing

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity (OTP).
  • To match candidates with recruiters and enable job applications/postings.
  • To process payments and activate subscriptions/wallet credit.
  • To provide support and respond to complaints.
  • To comply with legal obligations and keep the Service secure.

5. How We Protect Your Data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access, and secure storage. Payment processing is handled by our PCI-DSS-compliant online payment provider.

We share personal data only as needed to provide the Service, with:

9. Payments & the Payment Gateway

For payment transactions, our certified online payment provider acts as an independent controller of the personal data you provide during payment. The provider processes this data to carry out the payment, notify you of its status, handle complaints, and meet its legal obligations. Your rights regarding data processed by the payment provider (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability) can be exercised directly with them, and their privacy policy is available on the secure checkout page. This applies regardless of the language version of this website.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law (e.g. accounting records). You may request deletion at any time, subject to legal retention obligations.
