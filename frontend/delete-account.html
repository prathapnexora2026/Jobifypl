Delete your JobifyPL account

JobifyPL — operated by NEXORA TECH SOLUTIONS SP. Z O.O.

You can permanently delete your JobifyPL account and personal data at any time, directly from within the app. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

How to delete your account (in the app)

Job seekers (candidate):

  1. Open the JobifyPL app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Delete account”.
  4. Confirm on the popup — your account and data are removed immediately.

Recruiters:

  1. Open the JobifyPL app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (top-left) and tap “Delete account”.
  3. Confirm on the popup — your account and data are removed immediately.
No longer have the app? Email us from your registered email address at jobifyplsupport@jobifypl.pl with the subject “Account deletion request”, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove your personal data, including:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your profile and profile photo
  • Your uploaded CV and documents
  • Your job applications (job seekers) and job posts (recruiters)
  • Your wallet balance and notifications
  • Your login is disabled and cannot be used again

What data may be kept

For legal, accounting and tax reasons, we may retain anonymised transaction records (with your personal identifiers removed) for the period required by applicable Polish and EU law — typically up to 5 years. This retained data can no longer be linked back to you.

Last updated: 2026. For any questions about your data, contact jobifyplsupport@jobifypl.pl. See also our Privacy Policy.
